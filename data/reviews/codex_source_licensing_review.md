# Lane A source licensing review — 2026-10-05

Independent read-only review of official evidence found no licensing blocker
for the selected source records. This covers the licence scope recorded by the
bundles; it does not certify runtime collision, scale or functional behavior.

| Source | Verified evidence and scope |
| --- | --- |

No site-wide licence is substituted for the per-model designation. |

| Converted GSO | The [pinned conversion README](https://github.com/kevinzakka/mujoco_scanned_objects/blob/6ff8d275cebfd5b47e49685e3cfbe64b20e49a3c/README.md) specifies CC BY 4.0 for OBJ/PNG assets and MIT for MJCF XML. Converted directory IDs remain separate from official Google IDs. |

Individual XML classification remains unknown; the bundle records exact path identity and project-level evidence, with composition requirements. |
| Kenney | The [official pack page](https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit) and freshly downloaded bundled notice specify CC0. Five exact GLBs and the archive/licence hashes were verified directly; see `kenney_verification_2026-10-04.md`. Page and archive version labels are preserved separately. |

Verdict: accept the recorded source identities and licence framing. Contact
tasks remain proposed and require the separately reviewed execution proxies
and runtime manifests described in their plans.
