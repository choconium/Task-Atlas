# Free 3D asset sources for the next task rounds

- Accessed: 2026-09-25 (every fact below was checked on that date with curl, `gh api` or WebFetch; the URL checked is cited next to it)
- Generated by: LLM (claude-opus-5-5), not yet reviewed
- Sources already in use and out of scope: YCB, BEHAVIOR-1K, RoboCasa/Objaverse, Google Scanned Objects (GSO)

What YCB and GSO lack: both are **single rigid scanned meshes** of tabletop objects. They have no joints (drawers, doors, hinged lids, knobs, faucets), no appliances or furniture, and no room or apartment layouts. The main thing a new source can add is **articulation plus furniture/appliance/scene context**.

## 1. PartNet-Mobility (SAPIEN)

- **License.** Custom "SAPIEN PartNet-Mobility Terms of Use". The terms state: "Researcher shall use the Database only for non-commercial research and educational purposes." For-profit employers are also bound by the terms. The dataset "builds upon ShapeNet", and users "agree to abide by the ShapeNet terms of use". **No commercial use.** Attribution means citing the SAPIEN paper. Checked on the HF gated prompt: `https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/sapien-sim/PartNetMobility` (`cardData.extra_gated_prompt`, `license: other`). The same text appears in search results for https://sapien.ucsd.edu/about.
- **Access.** HF dataset `sapien-sim/PartNetMobility`, `gated: manual`. The request form asks for name, affiliation, purpose and a "non-commercial use ONLY" checkbox. Academic email domains are "typically approved automatically", while generic email providers "may be rejected". File downloads return 401 without access (tested on `.../resolve/main/7128.zip`). The original site `sapien.ucsd.edu` (and `storage1.ucsd.edu`, same IP 137.110.161.115) **refused connections** (ECONNREFUSED on port 443) on the access date.
- **Identifier.** An integer model ID, e.g. `7128`. Each one is a single file `<id>.zip` in the HF repo, so a stable ref is `https://huggingface.co/datasets/sapien-sim/PartNetMobility/blob/main/7128.zip` (the page exists but content is gated). The historical browse UI is https://sapien.ucsd.edu/browse, which I could not reach.
- **Catalog without login.**
  - The HF API file listing is public even though the files are gated: `GET https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/sapien-sim/PartNetMobility` gives 2,350 siblings = **2,347 `<id>.zip`** + README + metadata.json. `metadata.json` itself returns 401.
  - Category labels: `r-pad/partnet_mobility_utils` (third party) ships `src/rpad/partnet_mobility_utils/data_lists/all_ids.txt` = **2,347 `id,Category` lines** and 46 per-category files. `well_formed.txt` (2,218) and `missing_objs.txt` are that project's own quality filters. Endpoint: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/r-pad/partnet_mobility_utils/main/src/rpad/partnet_mobility_utils/data_lists/all_ids.txt`.
- **Counts.** 2,347 models in **46 categories**, from r-pad lists. Largest: StorageFurniture 346, Table 101, Faucet 84, Fan 81, Chair 81, TrashCan 70, Switch 70, Toilet 69, Eyeglasses 65, Cart 61, Globe 61, Window 58, Dispenser 57, Bottle 57, Laptop 55, CoffeeMachine 54, Dishwasher 48, Refrigerator 44, Door 36, Oven 30, Kettle 29, Toaster 25, KitchenPot 25, WashingMachine 17, Microwave 16.
- **Articulation.** Yes, this is the source's purpose: every model is a URDF with revolute/prismatic joints and part semantics. ManiSkill's faucet meta, for example, labels links as `hinge/switch` and `faucet_base` (from `mani_skill/assets/partnet_mobility/meta/info_faucet_train.json`).
- **Task families it adds.**
  - Opening and closing drawers and cabinet doors
  - Loading and unloading a **dishwasher** (rack and door) and a **washing machine**
  - Opening a fridge, microwave or oven and placing things inside
  - Turning a faucet handle on or off
  - Flipping light switches
  - Pressing a toaster lever
  - Operating a pump or soap dispenser
  - Opening and closing a toilet lid or seat
  - Opening a window or a room door
  - Opening a trash-can lid, a box flap, a kettle lid or a pot lid
  - Opening a laptop lid
  - Opening a safe door

  GSO/YCB cannot express any of these.

## 2. ReplicaCAD (Habitat 2.0)

- **License.** **CC BY 4.0**: "All materials are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) Public License." Commercial use is allowed. Attribution means citing Szot et al., NeurIPS 2021. Checked at https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai-habitat/ReplicaCAD_dataset/raw/main/README.md; the HF API also shows `license: cc-by-4.0`, `gated: False`.
- **Access.** Open download with no login (132 MB interactive version). A baked-lighting variant is at `ai-habitat/ReplicaCAD_baked_lighting`. ManiSkill mirrors it as `haosulab/ReplicaCAD`, also CC BY 4.0 and open.
- **Identifier.** The object config handle, e.g. `frl_apartment_bowl_01`. Stable URL: `https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai-habitat/ReplicaCAD_dataset/blob/main/configs/objects/frl_apartment_bowl_01.object_config.json` (HTTP 200). Articulated furniture example: `urdf/fridge/fridge.urdf`. Scenes are named `apt_0`…`apt_5` plus `v3_sc*_staging_*`.
- **Catalog without login.** `GET https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/ai-habitat/ReplicaCAD_dataset` lists 602 files. Of those, **92 `configs/objects/*.object_config.json`**, **12 URDFs** (6 unique furniture types plus `_dynamic` variants) and **91 `*.scene_instance.json`**.
- **Counts.** 92 objects: bowls, cups, plates, books, shoes, cloths, kitchen utensils, a pan, a chopping board, a sponge, a basket, bins, cushions, lamps and more. The README describes 6 FRL apartment recreations and 84 (of 105) furniture re-arrangements.
- **Articulation.** Yes, 6 furniture types; I counted the movable joints in each URDF:
  - fridge: 2 revolute
  - kitchenCupboard_01: 8 revolute
  - kitchen_counter: 7 prismatic
  - chestOfDrawers_01: 6 prismatic
  - cabinet: 2 prismatic
  - door1 (1 revolute) and doubledoor (2 revolute)

  There are also receptacle metadata for placing clutter.
- **Task families it adds.**
  - **Apartment-scale rearrangement and tidying across rooms**: put a bowl from the living-room table into a kitchen drawer
  - Fetching from the fridge
  - Setting and clearing the table in a full apartment
  - Opening a drawer and then storing an item in it

  Every object has a fixed, known home in a real apartment layout, which GSO lacks.

## 3. AI2-THOR / ProcTHOR object types

- **License.** **Apache-2.0** for `allenai/ai2thor`, `allenai/procthor` and `allenai/procthor-10k`, checked with `gh api repos/allenai/<repo> --jq .license.spdx_id`. Commercial use is allowed. Attribution follows Apache NOTICE rules; cite the papers. ManiSkill's repackaging `haosulab/AI2THOR` is labeled `cc-by-4.0` on HF. I found no separate asset license in the repos. The Apache terms appear to cover the Unity assets, but **this should be confirmed before asset redistribution**.
- **Access.** Open (GitHub, pip). Assets live inside the Unity build, so there is **no per-object mesh download page**. Meshes can be exported through ManiSkill's `ai2thor.zip` on HF.
- **Identifier.** `assetId` within an `objectType`, e.g. `Alarm_Clock_1` (type `AlarmClock`). Stable reference: the asset-database file below plus the type anchor at https://ai2thor.allenai.org/ithor/documentation/objects/object-types/.
- **Catalog without login.**
  - `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/allenai/procthor/main/procthor/databases/asset-database.json` contains **109 object types, 1,653 assets**, each with bbox, `primaryProperty`, `secondaryProperties` and split.
  - The iTHOR object-types docs table lists **125 types**, parsed from the HTML.
- **Articulation and state.** Simulator-level states, not URDF. Property counts across the 1,653 assets: CanOpen 186, CanToggleOnOff 281, CanBeSliced 180, CanBeFilled 127, CanBeDirty 197, CanBeCooked 30, isHeatSource 90.
  - Openable types include Fridge (30 assets), Microwave (30), Box (34), Laptop (31), Safe, Toilet, WashingMachine, ClothesDryer, Blinds, ShowerCurtain and Doorway.
  - Toggleable types include Faucet, LightSwitch, Toaster (30), CoffeeMachine (30), Television, lamps and WashingMachine.
  - Cabinet, Drawer, StoveKnob and StoveBurner exist in the iTHOR docs but are not in the ProcTHOR asset DB; they are baked into room prefabs.
- **Task families it adds.**
  - **Object-state goals**: slice, cook on a burner, fill a mug at the faucet, clean a dirty plate, switch the lights off, turn on the coffee machine
  - Multi-room ProcTHOR house tidying

  This source is strongest for defining goal predicates (open/closed, on/off, dirty/clean, filled, sliced). It is weak for realistic physical articulation.

## 4. HSSD (Habitat Synthetic Scenes Dataset)

- **License.** **CC BY-NC 4.0**, per the `license: cc-by-nc-4.0` card field and the gated prompt "You agree to use this dataset under the CC BY-NC 4.0 license" (https://huggingface.co/datasets/hssd/hssd-hab/raw/main/README.md). **No commercial use**; attribution required.
- **Access.**
  - `hssd/hssd-hab` (Habitat-ready): API shows `gated: False`, and I downloaded files without login. For example, `semantics/objects.csv` and `objects/0/<id>.object_config.json` returned HTTP 200/206.
  - `hssd/hssd-models` (raw assets): `gated: auto`, meaning an HF login plus clicking accept.
- **Identifier.** A 40-hex-character hash, e.g. `0001fb06b075a743e6289236cf049df3ad5dfa9c`. URL: `https://huggingface.co/datasets/hssd/hssd-hab/blob/main/objects/0/<id>.object_config.json`. Scenes are numeric, e.g. `102343992`.
- **Catalog without login.** `https://huggingface.co/datasets/hssd/hssd-hab/resolve/main/semantics/objects.csv` has **17,503 rows** with fields name, wnsynsetkey, main_category, super_category and **isArticulatable**. The HF API listing shows 16,539 object configs, 168 scene instances and 1,519 URDFs.
- **Counts.** README: "211 scenes and more than 18000 models". 86 `main_category` values, of which 3,941 rows are blank.
- **Articulation.** 2,108 objects flagged articulatable, with 1,519 URDFs. Mostly cabinet 582, chest_of_drawers 471, table 228, wardrobe 168, counter 148, fridge 57, washer_dryer 25, microwave 14, dishwasher 11. There is also an articulated scene config (`hssd-hab-articulated.scene_dataset_config.json`).
- **Task families it adds.** Large, realistic **whole-house scenes** for cross-room tidying and object search. There are many furniture articulations (wardrobes, dressers), and `metadata/affordance_objects.csv` offers affordance hints such as "turned on or off", "cleaned under a faucet" and "filled with water".
- **Limitation.** Non-commercial, and it overlaps ReplicaCAD/PartNet-Mobility for articulation.

## 5. OmniObject3D

- **License.** **CC BY 4.0**: "The OmniObject3D dataset is released under the CC BY 4.0" (README via `gh api repos/omniobject3d/OmniObject3D/readme`). Commercial use is allowed; attribution means citing the CVPR 2023 paper.
- **Access.** **Login required.** You must "Sign up" at OpenXLab (sso.openxlab.org.cn), then `openxlab login` with AK/SK, then `openxlab dataset get --dataset-repo omniobject3d/OmniObject3D-New` (~1.2 TB). A Google Drive mirror is linked from the README. HF has several **unofficial** mirrors, e.g. `AlbertS666/OmniObject3D-objects`, which contains only zip files and no listing.
- **Identifier.** `<category_name>/<object_id>` folders under `raw_scans/`, per the README tree; I did not verify a concrete example ID.
- **Catalog without login.** **None found.** The OpenXLab page is a 2.6 KB JS shell, and the guessed API endpoint returned 404.
- **Counts.** "6,000 scanned objects in 190 daily categories" (https://omniobject3d.github.io/).
- **Articulation.** None (static scans).
- **Task families it adds.** Mostly a GSO-like breadth expansion: fruit, vegetables, toys, dolls, stationery. It adds category diversity for pick/sort/pack, not new task families.

## 6. HOPE (Household Objects for Pose Estimation)

- **License.** The repo README says: "Copyright (C) 2021 NVIDIA Corporation … Licensed under the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license" (`gh api repos/swtyree/hope-dataset/readme`). The BOP HF card `bop-benchmark/hope` says `license: cc-by-sa-4.0`. **The two conflict; treat it as NC-SA (non-commercial, share-alike)** unless NVIDIA clarifies.
- **Access.** Open. Google Drive via `gdown`, or BOP on HF (`hope_models.zip`, not gated).
- **Identifier.** BOP-style `obj_0000NN` numbering in `hope_models.zip`. I did not open the zip. The README names the physical products: Melissa & Doug grocery boxes, cans, fridge fillers and condiments.
- **Catalog without login.** Only the README text ("28 toy grocery objects") and the HF file listing (13 files).
- **Counts.** 28 objects.
- **Articulation.** None.
- **Task families it adds.** Nothing new beyond YCB/GSO grocery tasks. Its one real advantage is that the physical objects **can be bought as a set (~60 USD, Sept 2021)**, which helps real-world reproducibility.

## 7. ManiSkill assets

- **License.** ManiSkill code is Apache-2.0 (`gh api repos/haosulab/ManiSkill`). The assets are **repackaged from upstream sources and keep those licenses**: YCB, PartNet-Mobility (non-commercial), ReplicaCAD (CC BY 4.0), AI2THOR (HF card cc-by-4.0), RoboCasa, OakInk-v2. The source list is in `mani_skill/utils/assets/data.py` (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/haosulab/ManiSkill/main/mani_skill/utils/assets/data.py).
- **Access.** Open URLs, no login: HF `haosulab/*`, and `storage1.ucsd.edu/datasets/ManiSkill2022-assets/partnet_mobility/dataset/<id>.zip`, which was unreachable today. This means ManiSkill ships PartNet-Mobility models **without the gate**, but the PartNet terms still apply to their use.
- **Identifier.** `partnet_mobility/<id>`. Note that ManiSkill's PartNet IDs (e.g. 1000–1100 for cabinets, 5000+ for faucets, 4000+ for buckets) are **a separate ID space** from the HF/SAPIEN 2,347 IDs. None of the IDs I sampled (1000, 1004, 5001, 4000) appear in r-pad `all_ids.txt`.
- **Catalog.** `mani_skill/assets/partnet_mobility/meta/info_{cabinet_drawer,cabinet_door,chair,bucket,faucet}_train.json` holds **25 + 42 + 26 + 29 + 60 = 182 entries**, with scale, bbox and link semantics. Plus data groups for ReplicaCAD, ReplicaCADRearrange, AI2THOR and RoboCasa.
- **Role.** This is a **delivery channel with sim-ready scaling and link semantics**, not a new object source. It is useful for making PartNet-Mobility, ReplicaCAD and AI2THOR tasks directly runnable.

## 8. Extra sources found

### 8a. Poly Haven models

- **License.** **CC0**: "Our assets are all licensed as CC0 … including commercial purposes" (https://polyhaven.com/license). No attribution is required.
- **Access and catalog.** Open JSON API, no key. `GET https://api.polyhaven.com/assets?t=models` returns **521 models**. `https://api.polyhaven.com/categories/models` returns category counts: props 176, furniture 85, containers 68, tools 70, electronics 31, appliances 22, food 19, shelves 16, dishes 13, office 12, vases 11, books 3.
- **Identifier.** Slug, e.g. `ArmChair_01`. Page: `https://polyhaven.com/a/ArmChair_01` (200). Files: `https://api.polyhaven.com/files/ArmChair_01`.
- **Articulation.** None (15 tagged "rigged").
- **Adds.** Clean, commercially free kitchen/dish/tool props and furniture for scene dressing. Small, and the collection skews toward outdoor and industrial models.

### 8b. Amazon Berkeley Objects (ABO)

- **License.** **CC BY 4.0**, and "Credit for the data, including all images and 3d models, must be given to: Amazon.com" (https://amazon-berkeley-objects.s3.amazonaws.com/3dmodels/README.md).
- **Access and catalog.** Public S3 bucket.
  - The listing `https://amazon-berkeley-objects.s3.amazonaws.com/?list-type=2&prefix=3dmodels/original/` paginates to **7,953 GLBs**.
  - Metadata: `3dmodels/metadata/3dmodels.csv.gz` (7,953 rows with extents) and `listings/metadata/*.json.gz` (product_type).
- **Identifier.** ASIN, e.g. `B01N2PLWIL`. URL: `https://amazon-berkeley-objects.s3.amazonaws.com/3dmodels/original/L/B01N2PLWIL.glb`.
- **Counts.** Product types over the 3D-model listings (98 types): CHAIR 1,184, SOFA 859, RUG 817, WALL_ART 599, TABLE 584, LAMP 345, PILLOW 275, PLANTER 182, CABINET 137, SHELF 106, DRESSER 62, VASE 50, STORAGE_BOX 23 and more.
- **Articulation.** None.
- **Adds.** Real-product furniture and decor with true metric extents: rearranging pillows, straightening rugs, placing lamps and planters, furniture-level staging. Few hand-manipulable items.

## Ranked recommendation for the next 3 task rounds

1. **PartNet-Mobility.** This is the largest leap in task families: opening doors and drawers, loading and unloading appliances, faucets, switches, lids. None of these is possible with YCB/GSO. It has 46 categories with real joint URLs and a public ID + category catalog (HF listing + r-pad lists), and it is sim-ready through ManiSkill/SAPIEN.
   - **Constraint:** non-commercial research/education only, and downloads need manual HF approval (an institutional email helps).
   - The Atlas only cites IDs and proposes tasks, which is fine. Anyone who *uses* the assets for commercial model training must clear the terms first.
   - If that is a blocker, promote #2 and #3 and use HSSD's or ReplicaCAD's URDFs instead.
2. **AI2-THOR / ProcTHOR.** Apache-2.0 and a fully open machine-readable catalog (1,653 assets, 109 types) with explicit state properties (open, toggle, slice, fill, dirty, cook). It is the best source for **state-change goals** and multi-room houses. Caveats: assets are Unity-bound, per-asset URLs are weak, and the asset license should be confirmed before redistribution.
3. **ReplicaCAD.** CC BY 4.0, open, no login, and a small, fully listable catalog (92 objects, 6 articulated furniture types, 6 apartments + 84 layouts). It adds **apartment-scale tidying and rearrangement** with real receptacles (fridge, kitchen drawers, cupboards, chest of drawers). It is small, so it suits one round of scene-level tasks rather than many rounds.

Runners-up:
- **HSSD** (richest scenes and articulation, but CC BY-NC)
- **ABO / Poly Haven** (commercially clean, but static and furniture-heavy)
- **OmniObject3D** (CC BY but login-only, no catalog, static)
- **HOPE** (28 objects, NC-SA, no new families)
